# 2026 — HeroX and collaborations

## MAKO12

The first major external collaboration recorded here is with Marco Lavorgna.

MAKO12 proposed ROBOTART + GPR + surface-wave seismic for subsurface crack detection in embankment dams.

A structured search evaluated 115,248 configurations.

The proposal included a route to physical sandbox testing, blind localization and progressive validation.

## Evolution 2.0

A later HeroX submission proposed chemical communication using coupled Belousov–Zhabotinsky oscillators, blind transmission and predefined acceptance criteria.

The proposal targeted 32 distinguishable states.

No final wet-lab conclusion is currently recorded in the archive.

## Historical role

HeroX introduced externally defined problems, team submissions and outside evaluation constraints.
