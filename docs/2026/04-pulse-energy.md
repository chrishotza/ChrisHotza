## Pulse Energy

With Marco Lavorgna, Pulse Energy developed as a software/control platform for microgrids and BESS.

The architecture included power conversion, battery management, peak management, grid/island transition, thermal recovery, synchronization and cybersecurity.

## Pre-HIL

A documented software campaign executed 66,000 scenarios.

A real import-limit overshoot defect was identified and corrected, with zero regressions in the subsequent full replay.

The June 2026 brief identifies Chris Mendoza with software development / Pre-HIL validation and Marco Lavorgna with system architecture / external relations.

## Energy Field Security

The project entered an external collaboration phase involving Marco Lavorgna and David A. Moran, including submission work.

## Conscious Pulse

Pulse Energy later evolved into Conscious Pulse.
